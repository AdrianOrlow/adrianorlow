## Building

- **Property intelligence at Nearmap** - a world-leading platform that turns aerial imagery into data about
  properties. I work across product features, identity and access integrations, and CI pipelines.
- **[myPolitics](https://mypolitics.pl)** - the largest civic education platform in Poland. I started it at 16
  and grew it to 4.5M+ users with zero marketing spend, purely by word of mouth. Almost every candidate in the 2025
  presidential election took part. I'm now rebuilding it for the 2027 parliamentary elections.
- **[Gieniek](https://gi.org.pl/gieniek)** - the foundation's AI agent. It opens pull requests, writes copy and edits
  videos. It learns how the foundation works from skills I wrote for it, covering grants, internships, publishing and
  design. In its first week, it shipped more than I had planned for the next three months.
- **[glitchroom](https://github.com/AdrianOrlow/glitchroom)** - breaks JPEGs at the data level instead of applying a
  filter. It runs in the browser, works offline, sends no telemetry and makes every glitch reproducible.
  [Try it](https://glitchroom.orlow.me).
- **[Athena](https://github.com/gi-org-pl/athena)**, **[Console](https://github.com/gi-org-pl/console-app)** and
  **[tech standards](https://github.com/gi-org-pl/gi-tech-standards)** - the design system, internal tools and
  engineering rules behind the foundation's products.
